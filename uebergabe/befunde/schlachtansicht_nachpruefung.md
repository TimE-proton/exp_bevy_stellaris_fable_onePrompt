### 2. With mouse look toggled on (M) a click acts on the panel under the hidden pointer and on the scene at once; for egui windows this is new behaviour of the shared mechanism

- Urteil: **bestätigt**
- Schwere laut Prüfer: minor
- Schwere laut Gegenprüfer: minor
- Ort: `crates/game/src/pointer.rs:236`

**Beschreibung.** track_pointer declares a grabbed pointer to be over nothing ('!grabbed && ...', comment at pointer.rs:230), so WorldMouse hands every click to pick_on_click. egui knows nothing of the grab and still delivers the click to the widget under the frozen, invisible pointer. In M mode one click therefore does both. For the top bar this is the original symptom of issue 2 through a remaining path. For the egui windows (side panels, ship panel) it is a change of this diff: at 8206ed1 the guard not(egui_wants_any_pointer_input) was true over windows (table in finding 'robustheit 2'), so such a click did not reach the scene (derived from the old guard, old build not run). No test covers a grabbed pointer. All other cases of issue 2 held in a real window: clicks, wheel and right/middle drags on every campaign panel, bar, resize handle and window and on the battle top bar, side panels, ship panel, Bilanz and help left selection and camera alone, and the same inputs beside the interface still reached map and scene.

**Fehlerbild.** Practice battle, select a ship (ship panel opens), move the pointer onto 'Taktik' in the top bar, press M, left click: 'Taktik' toggles and the selection is dropped, the ship panel closes. Before/after: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-exp-bevy-stellaris-fable-onePrompt/3f2a4996-068c-56f0-93b7-939afbb9e098/scratchpad/spiel/pruefung/bedienung/fotos/m_c11_12.png. Same with the pointer parked on the body of the ship panel: the click closes the panel (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-exp-bevy-stellaris-fable-onePrompt/3f2a4996-068c-56f0-93b7-939afbb9e098/scratchpad/spiel/pruefung/bedienung/fotos/m_p02_03.png). By the same path a click meant for a ship presses 'Verlassen' or 'Rückzug befehlen' if the pointer happened to rest there when M was pressed.

**Vorschlag.** Decide ownership of a grabbed pointer by the place where it was frozen (if the cover owns that place, the click belongs to the interface only), or better keep pointer input away from egui while the cursor is grabbed. Add a whole-game test with CursorGrabMode::Locked over the top bar and over a window.

**Begründung des Gegenprüfers.** Confirmed by reproduction. No test sets a grab mode (CursorGrabMode appears only in pointer.rs:232). On X11 the lock falls back to 'confined' (bevy_winit winit_windows.rs:448-450), so the hidden pointer keeps moving; I verified on X11 only. The trigger is narrow (toggle mode plus a click while the hidden pointer is over the interface), hence minor, but it is issue 2's symptom through a path the shared mechanism deliberately leaves open.
